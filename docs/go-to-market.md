# Go-To-Market

## Positioning

This portfolio is the owned channel for Gerard Recinto's engineering and startup story. Its job is to route visitors toward GitHub projects, consulting conversations, and product experiments.

## Monetization Path

| Offer | Buyer |
|---|---|
| Portfolio site | Recruiters, founders, collaborators. |
| Consulting CTA | Platform teams needing CI/CD, GitOps, Terraform, Kubernetes, or AI DevOps help. |
| Product funnel | Visitors interested in DevOpsLedger, DevOps MCP, or coaching products. |
| Content hub | Future blog posts and launch announcements. |

## Launch Hooks

- "A portfolio should sell a point of view, not just list projects."
- "The strongest signal is measurable engineering impact plus product direction."
- "Every project needs a path from GitHub visitor to conversation."
